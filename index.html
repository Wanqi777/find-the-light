Find the Light interactive room

Explore a dim room to find three directly interactive lights. The desk lamp toggles with a click. The ceiling light needs a long press. The floor lamp turns on or off when its pull cord is dragged down. For keyboard access, use Enter or Space on the desk lamp and pull cord. Hold Enter or Space on the ceiling pendant until it reaches full charge.
